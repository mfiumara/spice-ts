BSIM3 (LEVEL=8/49): `VERSION=3.2.x` cards now simulate with the ngspice-47 bsim3v32 revisions (3.2, 3.2.2, 3.2.3, 3.2.4, selected like b3v32set.c, including LMLT) instead of failing with `UNSUPPORTED_MODEL_VERSION` (#594); 3.0/3.1 are still rejected. BSIM3 devices of every revision now follow `.options TEMP` (b3temp.c laws) instead of being rejected, and an effective `NQSMOD=1` (or 3.3.0 `ACNQSMOD=1`) is now rejected with `UNSUPPORTED_MODEL_PARAMETER` instead of being silently simulated as the quasi-static model (#692).
